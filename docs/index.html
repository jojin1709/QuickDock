Zero Distraction

Lives quietly at the edge until you need it.

Traditional desktop widgets clutter your wallpaper and hog system memory. QuickDock rests discreetly at the top-center bezel of every display. Approach with your cursor or hit Ctrl + Space to expand smoothly.

Multi-Monitor Ready

Whether you use a single laptop screen or a dual 4K multi-monitor workstation, QuickDock spawns an independent notch per display. The notch stays locked at top-center across all DPI scaling factors.

Complete Privacy

Your data never leaves your computer.

QuickDock contains zero analytics, zero AI wrappers, zero telemetry pings, and no cloud sign-ins. Clipboard history and settings are stored locally in an encrypted SQLite database on your device.

100% Offline Architecture

No license keys, no trial counters, no subscriptions, no paywalls. Built in C# and WinUI 3 with native Win32 interop for maximum speed and sub-millisecond response times.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is QuickDock really free?
Yes, 100% free and open source. There are no license keys, no trial periods, no paid tiers, and no advertisements.
What are the system requirements?
QuickDock requires Windows 11 (or Windows 10 version 19041+) and runs on 64-bit x64 systems. .NET 10 runtime is included in the self-contained package.
Does QuickDock support multiple monitors?
Yes. QuickDock automatically detects all connected monitors and positions an independent notch on each display. Cursor position determines which notch expands when invoking global hotkeys.
Does QuickDock collect telemetry or crash reports?
No. QuickDock does not connect to any telemetry server or external API. All data stays strictly on your local disk.
